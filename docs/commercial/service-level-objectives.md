# Service level objectives

These objectives are internal reliability targets and do not become a customer SLA without an executed commercial agreement.

- Monthly availability target: 99.9 percent for proposal viewing and workspace quoting, excluding announced maintenance and upstream identity-provider outages.
- Health-check interval: five minutes.
- SEV-1 acknowledgement target: thirty minutes during supported hours.
- SEV-2 acknowledgement target: four business hours.
- Initial recovery targets: 24-hour RPO and four-hour RTO, subject to a successful restore drill.
- Security-report acknowledgement: two business days.

Customer agreements must state support hours, service credits, excluded events, data location, subprocessor notice, termination export and deletion commitments.
